The Problem

Restaurants Are Drowning in Admin

The same questions, every day. The same phone calls. The same review notifications sitting unanswered. AI fixes this.

Booking Queries at Every Hour

Diners don't book during office hours. They check availability at 10pm on a Tuesday. Without 24/7 cover, you're losing tables to restaurants that respond faster.

"Can You Cater for Allergies?"

Allergen and dietary queries are high-volume and legally important. Staff errors cost you customers and, in serious cases, can have real consequences. AI answers these consistently and accurately.

Reviews Going Unanswered

Google and TripAdvisor reviews directly affect where you appear in local search. Unanswered reviews — especially negative ones — signal neglect. Most restaurants don't have time to respond to every one.

Staff Turnover and Rising Wages

Hospitality staff turnover runs at 30–40% annually. NLW rose to £12.21 in April 2025 and hits £12.71 in April 2026. AI handles the repeatable admin tasks so your people spend time on what actually matters.

AI Agents Built for Restaurants

Table Booking Agent

Menu & Allergen Query Agent

Trained on your full menu — ingredients, allergens, dietary options and daily specials. Answers customer questions instantly and accurately. Escalates any query it can't resolve to your team.

Review Response Agent

Monitors your Google and TripAdvisor listings. Responds to new reviews within hours — in your voice, professionally written. Consistent responses improve your local ranking and show diners you care.

WhatsApp & Social Inbox Agent

Handles incoming WhatsApp messages, Facebook Messenger and Instagram DMs. Replies to common questions, directs diners to your booking link and flags anything urgent to your team.

Sends booking reminders to reduce no-shows. Manages a waitlist automatically — when a cancellation comes in, the next diner in line is notified and confirmed within minutes.
