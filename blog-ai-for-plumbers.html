For a plumber or tradesperson, the phone is both your lifeline and your biggest problem. Every missed call is a potential job lost. But you can't answer your phone when you're on a job — and you shouldn't have to choose between doing good work and winning new work.

That's the problem AI agents solve. Not by replacing you, but by handling the admin layer — the enquiries, the quote follow-ups, the invoice reminders — so that every contact gets a fast, professional response even when you're on-site, driving, or finished for the day.

💡 Key insight: The jobs lost to "no answer" rarely come back. Customers with urgent plumbing issues — a dripping tap, a boiler fault, a blocked drain — go to whoever responds first. AI ensures that's always you.

The Missed Call Problem Every Tradesperson Recognises

The pattern is familiar: you finish a job, check your phone, and there are two missed calls from unknown numbers. By the time you ring back, one has already booked someone else. The other one doesn't pick up. That's potentially a hundred pounds or more — gone.

Evening and weekend enquiries are even worse. A homeowner discovers a leak at 7pm on a Friday. They message three plumbers on Google. The one who responds fastest gets the job. If you're not watching your messages, that job goes to a competitor — and so does the follow-on work when they need a bathroom fitting next spring.

An AI enquiry agent changes this completely. When someone messages you — via your website contact form, WhatsApp, or email — it replies within seconds, asks the right qualifying questions (what's the issue, where are you, when do you need it done?), and holds the conversation until you're free to take over.

What an AI Agent Does for a Plumbing Business

A well-configured AI agent for a UK plumber or tradesperson handles several distinct tasks:

  • Enquiry handling 24/7 — responds to every new message instantly, qualifies the job, and collects the customer's details
  • Quote follow-up — sends an automatic follow-up 24–48 hours after you send a quote, and again at 72 hours if there's no response. Most jobs won on second contact never get chased
  • Invoice chasing — sends polite payment reminders on day 3, day 7 and day 14 after invoice. No awkward calls. Professional, timed messages that get paid
  • Scheduling — books appointments into your diary, sends confirmation to the customer, and sends a reminder the day before
  • Out-of-hours capture — captures emergency enquiries overnight and at weekends, with an automatic acknowledgement so the customer knows you'll be in touch first thing

What Happens When a Customer Messages at 8pm

Here's a real-world scenario: a homeowner in your area discovers a slow leak under their kitchen sink on a Tuesday evening. They search Google, find your business, and send a WhatsApp message. Here's how the AI handles it:

  1. 1

    Message arrives at 8:14pm

    The AI responds within seconds: "Hi, thanks for getting in touch with [Your Business]. I can help — can you tell me a bit more about the issue? Is the leak active right now or is it a slow drip?"

  2. 2

    AI qualifies the job

    Asks the right questions — location, urgency, access, preferred timing. The customer feels heard. By the end of the exchange, you have a qualified lead with all the details you need to quote or book.

  3. 3

    Booking confirmed or morning callback arranged

    If the job is non-urgent, the AI books a time slot from your available diary. If it's an emergency, it sends an immediate acknowledgement and flags the job so it's the first thing you see in the morning.

  4. 4

    Reminder sent the day before

    The customer gets an automated reminder the afternoon before the appointment — reducing no-shows and keeping the job in their diary.

  5. 5

    Invoice sent and chased automatically

    After the job, the AI sends the invoice and follows up automatically if payment isn't received by day 7. No awkward chasing calls needed.

Common Questions from UK Tradespeople

My customers want to talk to a person, not a bot

They want a fast, helpful response. If a customer messages three plumbers and two are silent until the morning, the one that responds in seconds — even via a professional AI — wins the job. You can always follow up personally once you're free. The AI holds the customer and keeps them engaged until you can.

What if the AI gives the wrong price or books me in at the wrong time?

The agent is trained on your specific rates, coverage area, and availability. It doesn't guess. For anything outside its brief — unusual jobs, complex commercial work — it takes the customer's details and tells them you'll be in touch personally. You stay in control of pricing; the AI handles the admin around it.

I already handle my own admin — why pay £99/month?

The question isn't whether you can handle your own admin. It's what that time is worth. If you spend 30–40 minutes a day on messages, quotes and invoice chasing, that's roughly 15 hours a month of billable time that isn't being billed. At even £50/hour call-out rate, that's £750 a month in lost revenue — to save £99.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Quote follow-up is where most tradespeople leave money on the table. A customer who doesn't reply to your initial quote isn't necessarily saying no — they may just be busy, distracted, or waiting to see if another quote comes in lower. A single automated follow-up at 48 hours converts a significant proportion of those "silences" into jobs.
