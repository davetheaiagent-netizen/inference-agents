Executive Summary
The Opportunity in One Sentence

Inference Agents is building the UK's first WhatsApp AI agent with native OpenTable integration — enabling restaurants to accept, confirm and manage table bookings via WhatsApp 24/7 with zero staff involvement.

The UK restaurant sector processes millions of booking enquiries each year. The majority arrive outside business hours, during service, or when staff are too busy to respond. Independent restaurants lose an estimated 3–5 bookings per week to slow or missed responses — each worth £80–£200 in revenue.

Inference Agents solves this by deploying an AI WhatsApp agent trained on the restaurant's tone, FAQs and menu — integrated directly with their booking system. The AI handles the full conversation, checks live availability, and confirms the reservation instantly. No staff. No delays. No missed bookings.

Conversational commerce is the category this product sits within — the use of messaging apps, AI chatbots and voice assistants to complete transactions conversationally. It is one of the fastest growing segments in the broader AI automation market, driven by consumer preference for WhatsApp over phone calls and the availability of capable AI models.

The restaurant booking niche within conversational commerce is underserved in the UK. Voice AI (phone-based) has seen early adoption (PolyAI, MillieAI), but WhatsApp-native booking with real-time booking system integration does not yet exist as a standalone product at the SME price point.

The Full Stack Differentiator

The Full Stack tier is the product no UK competitor currently offers at this price point: a WhatsApp AI that checks live table availability and makes the booking in the restaurant's existing system (OpenTable, ResDiary or Mews) in real time. This eliminates the human confirmation step entirely and is the product that justifies premium pricing.

Technical stack: Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp) → Cloudflare Pages Functions (serverless) → Claude Sonnet 4.6 (AI) → OpenTable Partner API (booking) → Supabase (logging) → Resend (confirmations). Zero third-party middleware. Near-zero infrastructure cost per customer.
