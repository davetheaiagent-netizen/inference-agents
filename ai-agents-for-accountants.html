The Problem

Admin Is Eating Your Practice's Profits

The most valuable hours in an accountancy practice are the ones spent giving advice — not chasing documents, sending reminders, or answering the same client questions.

Client Onboarding Takes Weeks Instead of Days

Chasing clients for ID documents, engagement letters, bank statements and previous-year figures is slow and manual. Every delay pushes back your workflow and frustrates clients before you've even started.

Invoice Chasing Is Awkward and Often Ignored

Most practices struggle to collect fees consistently. Chasing clients for payment feels uncomfortable, so it gets put off. Late payments pile up and cash flow suffers.

Compliance Deadlines Need Constant Monitoring

HMRC deadlines, VAT quarters, corporation tax due dates, confirmation statements — missing one costs your clients money and damages your reputation. Manual tracking in spreadsheets isn't reliable enough.

Routine Queries Fill Your Inbox Every Day

"When is my tax return due?" "What do I need to send you?" "Can I see my last invoice?" Your team answers these questions on repeat — hours that could be spent on advisory work instead.
