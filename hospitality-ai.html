The Problem

Bookings are leaking out of WhatsApp

Guests message on WhatsApp because it's where they live. But restaurants can't staff a real-time messaging service 24/7.

Messages after 10pm go unanswered

Your Saturday night enquiry arrives at 10:30pm. You reply Tuesday morning. They booked somewhere else on Sunday. That's a table for 4 at £50/head — gone.

Staff can't monitor WhatsApp during service

Your front-of-house team is on the floor. Nobody is watching the phone. "We checked messages at the end of the night" means you're always a day behind.

Enquiries that don't convert to bookings

Even when you do reply, the back-and-forth — date, time, party size, availability — takes 6 messages and an hour. Half of guests give up and book somewhere easier.

No record of what was discussed

Dietary requirements, special occasions, pre-orders — mentioned in WhatsApp, never makes it into OpenTable. Guests arrive for a birthday dinner and nothing's been set up.

💬

The fix: WhatsApp AI that connects to your booking system

Our AI responds instantly to every WhatsApp message, collects the details, checks live availability in your booking system, and confirms the reservation — all inside the conversation. Your team wakes up to a full diary, not a backlog of unanswered messages.

How It Works

Three steps, fully automated

From first message to confirmed booking — without your team touching a phone

1

Guest messages on WhatsApp

They ask about availability, a special occasion, group size — whatever's on their mind. The AI responds in under 3 seconds, any time of day or night, in a natural conversational tone that matches your restaurant's personality.

2

AI checks your live booking system

Once the AI has date, time, and party size, it queries OpenTable or ResDiary in real time. It only offers slots that are actually available — no double-bookings, no awkward follow-up corrections.

3

Reservation confirmed, guest delighted

The AI creates the booking directly in your system and sends the guest a confirmation in WhatsApp — with the booking reference, any notes about special requirements, and a reminder the day before.
