The Problem

Healthcare Admin Is Consuming Clinical Time

Every hour spent on admin and booking is an hour not spent on patients. AI fixes that.

No-Shows Are Killing Your Revenue

The average UK clinic loses 25–35% of appointment revenue to no-shows and late cancellations. Without automated reminders and confirmation sequences, every gap in your diary is lost income.

Receptionists Overwhelmed With Booking Calls

Phone lines clogged with appointment requests, rescheduling and FAQs means reception staff can't do anything else. And when they're busy, calls go unanswered — and patients book elsewhere.

Reviews Going Unmanaged

A Google rating below 4.5 stars loses you patients before they even make contact. Most practices don't have time to respond to reviews consistently, missing a critical trust signal.

Rising Staff Costs, Shrinking Margins

NLW increases and employer NIC rises mean reception and admin roles cost significantly more than two years ago — with no increase in the revenue they generate.
