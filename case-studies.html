DNA Rate Drops from 18% to 4% in Eight Weeks

NHS & private dental practice, South London · Clinic Package £199/mo

A busy 4-chair dental practice was losing thousands of pounds every month to did-not-attend appointments — patients forgetting, not confirming, or simply not replying to a single reminder email sent the evening before.

We deployed an AI Reminder & Follow-Up agent that sends a sequence of SMS and email reminders at 7 days, 48 hours and 2 hours before each appointment. The agent also handles new patient enquiries via the website, collects basic health history data, and sends post-appointment care instructions automatically.
