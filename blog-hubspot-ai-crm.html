How to Build an Automated Sales Pipeline for Under £200/Month

Most UK small businesses have no real sales process. Leads come in, some get followed up, most don't. Here's how to fix that — completely automatically — for less than £200/month.

The Problem With Most Small Business Sales

Ask most small business owners how their sales process works and you'll get something like: "A lead comes in, I try to get back to them, sometimes I follow up, sometimes I forget."

That's not a sales process — it's hope. And hope doesn't scale.

The businesses growing fastest right now have something different: a system that catches every lead, follows up automatically, and keeps nudging until the prospect either buys or tells them to stop. The good news is that system now costs less than £200/month to run with AI.

The 4-Piece Stack

You only need four things:

  1. Lead capture — a form on your website (or your ROI calculator, contact page, or consultation booking page)
  2. Free CRM — HubSpot's free tier is genuinely excellent for small businesses
  3. AI sales agent — sends personalised follow-up emails automatically
  4. AI email sequencer — runs multi-step outreach without you lifting a finger

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Set Up HubSpot Free CRM

Go to hubspot.com and create a free account. Set up a pipeline with stages: New Lead → Contacted → Demo Booked → Proposal Sent → Won/Lost. This takes about 20 minutes and costs nothing. Every lead will flow through here.

2

Connect Your Lead Capture Forms

Any form on your website (contact page, ROI calculator, consultation booking) should feed directly into HubSpot. Use HubSpot's free form builder or connect your existing forms via Zapier. When a lead comes in, it auto-creates a contact in the CRM.

3

Set Up Your AI Sales Agent

The AI sales agent monitors HubSpot for new leads and sends the first personalised email within 5 minutes of the form submission. It uses the lead's name, what they enquired about, and your business context. Not a template — a genuinely personalised message.

4

Configure the Follow-Up Sequence

The AI email agent runs the drip sequence: Day 3 sends a relevant case study or piece of value. Day 7 asks if they have questions. Day 14 makes a soft offer or suggests a quick call. Day 21 is the final "closing the loop" message. All personalised, all automatic.

5

Add a Booking Link

Every email in the sequence includes a Calendly (or similar) link for booking a call. When someone books, it auto-updates their stage in HubSpot to "Demo Booked" and sends you a notification. You show up prepared with the lead's full history.

What the Running Pipeline Looks Like

Once this is set up, here's what happens every time a lead comes in — without you doing anything:

  • Lead submits your contact form at 11pm on a Sunday
  • Within 5 minutes, they receive a personalised reply from you (well, your AI)
  • They're added to HubSpot as a new lead
  • Day 3: they get a follow-up with a relevant example or case study
  • Day 7: a friendly "any questions?" message
  • Day 14: a clear offer to book a call
  • If they book: you get a notification, they get a confirmation, HubSpot updates
  • If they don't respond after day 21: they're moved to a "nurture" list for monthly updates

You only have to get involved when a call is booked. Everything else runs itself.

The conversion uplift is real: Businesses that follow up within 5 minutes of a lead enquiring are 9x more likely to convert them than those who follow up after 30 minutes. Automated follow-up sequences increase overall conversion rates by 30–50% on average.

How Long Does Setup Take?

We can have this fully live for your business within 48 hours. That includes configuring the AI with your tone of voice, connecting your forms, setting up the HubSpot pipeline, writing the follow-up sequence, and testing end-to-end.

After that, the system runs itself. The only maintenance is reviewing the monthly performance report and occasionally updating the copy if your offer changes.
