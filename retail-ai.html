The Problem

UK Retail Is Fighting on Every Front

Wages are rising, margins are tightening, and customer expectations have never been higher. AI changes the equation.

Rising Wage Costs, Tighter Margins

NLW at £12.21 (April 2025), rising to £12.71 in April 2026. Combined with employer NIC at 15%, every customer service hire costs significantly more than two years ago.

Customers Expect Amazon-Level Service

24/7 responses, instant order updates, same-day replies to complaints. Independent retailers can't staff that without huge costs.

Negative Reviews Going Unanswered

A single unanswered 1-star review on Google or Trustpilot can deter dozens of potential customers. Most retailers don't have time to respond consistently.

Abandoned Carts and Missed Follow-Ups

Up to 70% of online carts are abandoned. Without automated follow-up, that revenue is gone permanently.
